Delusion-mitigation training teaches models to stop amplifying delusional spirals. Existing metrics score the presence of metaphysical themes, so they can’t tell a model that stopped amplifying from one that stopped discussing spirituality at all. I’m designing a benchmark that separates the two: matched pathological and non-pathological spiritual scenarios, judged on discernment and support rather than topic avoidance. If the suppression is real, it’s a miscalibrated guardrail rather than a safety win: people who can’t talk about their experience with the model don’t stop having it, they route around the model.
