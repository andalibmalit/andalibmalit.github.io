For GSU labor-economics research, I parallelized a difference-in-differences, event-study, and triple-difference pipeline over ~323M job postings, replacing a 12+ hour Stata workflow with a 1–2 hour Python pipeline across a 251-way Slurm array. The research asks how generative AI's diffusion after ChatGPT reshaped firms' hiring demand across advanced and developing economies. The pipeline is in active use and resulted in the publication "Generative AI and Firm Hiring Demand: Evidence from Advanced and Developing Economies."
